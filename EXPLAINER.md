This app lets you control your computer with hand gestures in front of a webcam.
It watches your hand with a ready-made hand-tracking model and turns what your hand does into mouse and keyboard actions.
Point one finger to move the mouse, pinch to click, slide two fingers to scroll, and swipe an open palm to change slides.
You see yourself on screen with the detected gesture written on it, so you always know what it understood.
Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere — everything runs on your own computer.
